Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our credit mechanics, rollovers, and enterprise capabilities.

How do GridBeacon scan credits work?

expand_more
A scan credit represents 1 real-world geo-coordinate queried against Google Local algorithms. For instance, a 5×5 scan checks 25 pins (25 credits), a 9×9 checks 81 points (81 credits), and a 21×21 checks 441 points (441 credits). You only spend credits when a scan executes.

What happens if I don't use all my credits this month?

expand_more
They roll over automatically! Unused scan credits from your monthly plan allowance remain in your account as long as your subscription is active. They never expire, so you never lose the value of what you paid for.

Are competitor ranks charged extra?

expand_more
No! Unlike other platforms that bill you per tracked competitor or charge double to benchmark rival businesses, GridBeacon captures competitor grids and rank visibility from the same scan at zero additional credit cost.

Can I use my custom agency domain (CNAME)?

expand_more
Yes, on the Agency plan ($69.99/mo), you can point a custom subdomain like reports.youragency.com directly to GridBeacon. Your clients can log in with your branded portal, viewing reports with your colors, logos, and favicons, without any trace of GridBeacon.

Do you support international geo-scans?

expand_more
Yes. GridBeacon runs scans worldwide across all Google-supported localized search engines and languages (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, Latin America, and Asia). You can enter exact street addresses or latitude/longitude coordinates anywhere globally.
