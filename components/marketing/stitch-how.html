Frequently Asked Questions About Grid Telemetry

Everything you need to know about how GridBeacon measures local rankings accurately and safely.

How does GridBeacon get live rankings without getting blocked by Google?

We operate a distributed network of private, geo-located residential proxies. Each coordinate query passes accurate mobile viewport parameters and GPS coordinate headers identical to a real browser or Google Maps app search, guaranteeing fresh, unpersonalized, and authentic rankings.

Can I track competitor businesses without having access to their Google account?

Yes, 100%. GridBeacon operates completely from the consumer perspective. Because we simulate end-user searches across coordinates, you can track your own profile, your top local rival, or a national brand with no GBP authorization or login credentials required.

What is the difference between Visibility % and Average Rank?

Average Rank is the mathematical mean of your position across all grid coordinates. Visibility % is a weighted share-of-voice metric that represents what percentage of potential search traffic you capture based on ranking in the high-converting 3-Pack (positions 1-3).

How often should an agency run a geo-grid scan?

For competitive local niches (lawyers, roofers, cosmetic dentists), weekly scans provide the ideal balance between tracking algorithmic fluctuations and maintaining credit efficiency. For standard local SMBs, bi-weekly or monthly scans are generally sufficient to prove retainer value.

Do unused scan credits roll over if I am on a monthly subscription?

Yes. All active agency subscriptions include rollover capability for up to 3 billing cycles, ensuring your credits never vanish during slow seasonal periods.
